# novel-plan-next

## Purpose

Turn canon state into a concrete next-step plan.

Use `novel-load-context` first.

## Workflow

1. Load the chapter brief.
2. Choose one primary thread and at most two secondary pressures.
3. Produce a plan with:
   - goal
   - conflict
   - scene order
   - reveal or reversal
   - chapter-end hook
4. Keep the plan executable. If it cannot be drafted scene by scene, it is too vague.

## Guardrails

- Do not invent major setting changes that are absent from canon.
- Do not resolve every open thread in one chapter.
- Each plan must preserve at least one future hook.
