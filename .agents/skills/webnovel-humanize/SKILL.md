# webnovel-humanize

## Purpose

Lower AI smell without breaking story facts or drifting into direct imitation.

## Use It With

- Draft Queue
- Review Center
- Style Lab anti-AI focus items

Reference: `docs/local-codex-studio.md`

## Workflow

1. Identify the current anti-AI focus list from the active style profile.
2. Rewrite the draft with explicit attention to:
   - reducing explanation-heavy narration
   - varying sentence rhythm
   - moving emotion into action or sensory detail
   - sharpening scene exits and hook pressure
3. Preserve plot facts and scene order.
4. Send the result to `webnovel-review`.

## Output

- A revised draft with lower AI smell and preserved story intent.
